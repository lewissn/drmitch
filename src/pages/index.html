Questions

Before you book

Why does he use the title “Dr”?

“Dr” is a courtesy title available to chiropractors registered with the General Chiropractic Council. Mitch is a Doctor of Chiropractic. He is not a medical doctor and does not hold a general medical qualification.

Do I need a GP referral?

No, you can book directly. With your permission, Mitch can write to your GP about your care.

How long are appointments?

A new patient consultation is {{newMin}} minutes. Follow-up treatments are {{followMin}} minutes, and a sports massage is {{massageMin}} minutes.

What should I wear?

Something comfortable you can move in. Shorts are useful if it’s your hips, knees or legs.
